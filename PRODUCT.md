# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Small-business owners in India (retail, textiles, wholesale; shopkeepers) and whoever keeps their books. Confirmed: used on both phone (quick capture mid-day, between customers) and desktop (evening/monthly review) with equal weight. Mixed English/Hindi comfort.

## Product Purpose

DHAN ("Know your money. Grow your business.") turns everyday transactions into clear financial decisions: dashboard of income/expenses/cash balance, rule-based insights and anomaly alerts, a 30-day cash forecast, and an indicative credit-readiness score. Built for Hack2Ignite 2026 (PS ID FT-05).

## Positioning

Cash-flow clarity plus a transparent, explainable credit-readiness signal for businesses that lack formal credit history. Every number is computed from the user's own data and its method is disclosed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 18, Vite, Tailwind, TanStack Query, Recharts, Framer Motion; FastAPI + MongoDB backend.
- Manual and OCR bill entry; DEMO vs LIVE data tagging; credit score is explicitly not a CIBIL score and must always show its disclaimer.
- UI language: English with a Hindi toggle this round (Devanagari-capable type; INR in lakh/crore grouping).
- Budgets, Reports, full Settings not yet implemented.

## Brand Commitments

Name DHAN; tagline "Know your money. Grow your business." Direction confirmed with the user: evolve the existing trust-first navy + green identity rather than replace it.

## Evidence on Hand

No real customers, testimonials or benchmarks exist; demo data is synthetic and must stay labelled.

## Product Principles

1. Honest numbers: never show a figure without its basis or a disclaimer where it is indicative.
2. Glanceable first, detail on demand: the owner should know "am I okay?" in two seconds.
3. Capture must be effortless; analysis can be rich.
4. Respect the user's language and number conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mixed-literacy users and bright shop lighting: high contrast, large touch targets, no color-only meaning, reduced-motion respected.
